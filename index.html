Hi, I'm Misha – I develop and design electronic hardware that creates awesome, useful hardware.

I graduated with a degree in electronics engineering and am seeking a junior or graduate role in electronics engineering where I can work on PCB and schematic capture design, hardware development, embedded coding & debugging, soldering & PCB assembly, and verification of electronic circuits. Where I fit

I am looking for junior roles in electronic circuit design, hardware prototyping, embedded firmware development, testing and verification, and PCB design and manufacturing.

Technical strengths

Design electronics – Design printed circuit boards based on realistic designs, turning them into electronic solutions for product development on a custom basis and for my portfolio. Use KiCAD and Altium Designer to capture schematics and PCB designs.

Prototyping electronics – Prototype using an evaluation board, an own-designed PCB, and willing to learn new evaluation boards to help sort out different integrated circuits, such as battery-fuel-gauging management, sensors, and other unusual integrated circuit-based devices.

Embedded software – Coding and debugging for microcontroller and embedded applications with register-level code, hardware abstraction level, official software development kits, and real-time operating systems.

Software Development – Develop lightweight desktop software for everyday use and requirements for complex project solutions with programming languages C, C++, C#, Python and Rust.

Fabricating, assembling PCB – soldering and reflowing surface-mount electronic components up to 0201 size for passive components, as well as QFN-80 and SOT-5X3 size integrated circuits, with a total component count of over 150 pieces per single PCB.

Troubleshooting and verification PCB – finding faults in the PCB by checking schematics documentation, replacing faulty electronic components, rewriting tracks of PCB for prototyping, and checking track continuity and logging the issue of PCB for future re-designing PCB and schematics.

Transferable strengths

Teamwork – Participate in teams to help develop electronics hardware with the right design specification, and specify design electronics and mechanical constraints with communication between different engineering disciplines.

Learning – Exploring new engineering disciplines and skills, new programming languages, manufacturing and fabrication skills, and applying new technologies and new algorithms of machine learning, new protocols and safety requirements of product development.
